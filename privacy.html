Video Engine – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Video Engine is a private tool run by Savest (savest.no). It creates calm music videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by Savest. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

When a channel owner signs in with Google, Video Engine asks for permission to upload videos and read basic channel information through the YouTube API Services. It uses this access only to:

  • show which YouTube channel is connected,
  • upload and schedule videos that the owner has approved,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags and privacy settings on those videos.

How the data is stored

Access tokens and video information are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server and are not sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

You can remove Video Engine's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all uploads. Locally stored tokens are deleted when a channel is disconnected in the app.

Google and YouTube

Video Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/oliversaugestad-prog/oliversaugestad-prog.github.io or contact Savest through savest.no.
